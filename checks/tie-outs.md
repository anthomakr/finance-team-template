# Tie-out checks

Every close passes these before sign-off. A failed check stops the close; it is explained, never patched.

| # | Check | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Balance sheet: total assets minus total liabilities and equity | 0, every period |
| 2 | Cash: closing cash in the cash flow statement minus cash on the balance sheet | 0, every period |
| 3 | Net income: P&L net income minus the movement in retained earnings (net of dividends) | 0 |
| 4 | Bank: ledger bank balance minus bank statement balance, per account | 0, or listed reconciling items |
| 5 | Trial balance: total debits minus total credits | 0 |
| 6 | Intercompany: receivable at entity A minus payable at entity B, per pair | 0 |
| 7 | Opening balances: this year's opening balance sheet minus last year's closing | 0 |
| 8 | Monthly vs annual: sum of the 12 months minus the annual figure, per P&L line | 0 |
